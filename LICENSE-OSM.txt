Road geometry attribution and licence

demo_roads.geojson contains adapted OpenStreetMap road data, clipped to the
Paya Rumput boundary. Road data copyright OpenStreetMap contributors, available
under the Open Data Commons Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0.

Attribution: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
Licence: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

The adapted road database is supplied under ODbL 1.0. Preserve attribution and
follow the licence when redistributing this road database or derived databases.
Each feature includes an OpenStreetMap way URL; metadata includes the source
snapshot timestamp and selection criteria. The database is provided as GeoJSON
so it remains available in a machine-readable form.

The traffic scores are a separate fictional simulation, not OpenStreetMap
traffic data. Boundary geometry is separately sourced from ElectionData.MY.
